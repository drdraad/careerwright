# Careerwright: three-minute demo

For 5 November 2026. A three-minute walkthrough of the integrated app.
**ACTION** tells you what to do on screen. **SAY** is the exact spoken script.
Everything outside the SAY blocks is a presenter note, not narration.

### Where Muse is used — presenter reference

| Part of the product | Muse's role | What this take shows |
|---|---|---|
| Conversation and profile | Ask questions and propose structured answers for the person to accept or reject. | A saved real Muse reply, followed by the two accepted answers. |
| Career suggestions and explanations | Propose directions and explain possible matches using the person's answers and occupation records. The app checks proposed titles and quoted passages. | Described during Roles; no new discovery call or claim that P01's prepared target was generated by Muse. |
| Strength suggestions | Propose a strength with a quotation from the person's own words. The app checks the quotation; the person confirms the suggestion. | Described during Skills; P01's saved examples remain self-reported evidence. |
| Role, course and funding research | Search for source pages and, where structured page data is unavailable, extract quoted facts. The app checks quotations against the fetched text. | Described during Options while inspecting sources. P01's prepared records are not presented as a fresh Muse research result. |
| Route comparison and planning | The app calculates readiness, time, costs and feasibility from saved inputs. Muse does not calculate the route ranking or decide which plan to save. | The existing computed P01 routes and saved Route 2 plan. |

The default model for live workspaces is `muse-spark-1.3`. The saved synthetic interview
uses its Contributor variant. `muse-wonder-1.0` is supported for explicitly configured
owner sessions; it is not the model behind this saved reply. P01 uses prepared synthetic
data and offline demo behavior. Only the opening interview demonstrates saved real Muse
output; the other model uses are described, not run during the recording.

## Fallback

- **A page fails to load:** show its prepared tab and say it was opened earlier. Do not
  disguise an error as an empty result or claim a new save succeeded.
- **Route details are still loading:** keep the labelled pending result visible. Use the
  previously opened saved Plan; do not start paid research to fill the pause.
- **Running late:** omit the Options source disclosure and Plan reload. Preserve the
  unchecked-figure qualification, unresolved SQL and practical next action. Finish by 3:00.

## Claims to keep bounded

Demo Roles has no sourced vacancies. P05 lacks enough modelled evidence to claim “ready to
apply”. P03's possible grant and bridge income are unmodelled. Optional Roles research has
no persisted reviewed decision. A complete planning description is distinct from checked
sources, feasibility, conditions and reported completion. Changed or expired research can
make routes and plans stale. A modelled route does not guarantee a place, funding or work.
“Cheapest and shortest” refers to the options, constraints and time horizon included in
the monthly model. The app shows trade-offs between cost and time; one route need not win
on both. A stopped or truncated search does not establish a global optimum.
