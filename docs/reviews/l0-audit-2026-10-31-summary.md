# Your decisions after the audit (31 October 2026)

Answer each with yes or no. It takes about 10 minutes.
Reply like this: "1 yes, 2 yes, 3 no …". Add a note to any of them.

## Where things stand

- **Works:** the You chapter. You can talk, get career ideas with reasons, and choose a target.
- **Not built yet:** Skills, Options, Routes and Plan.
- **Biggest gap:** skills, options and routes work for only 4 example jobs. Pick any other job
  and the journey stops.
- **Roles are limited to the European job list (ESCO)** by our own rule, not by the original
  design.
- **A UK list of about 24,000 job titles is already in the app,** unused.

## What gets built

1. **Build the working steps first, and the special effects after.**
   - Yes: Skills, Options, Routes and Plan come next, built plainly in today's look.
   - The world's extra animations wait until those steps work.
   - "Every score at least 4" becomes the bar for release, not for starting each chapter.

2. **Recognise any UK job title, and always keep yours.**
   - Yes: the first question also searches the UK list.
   - Your own words are always accepted, and you can say "None of these is my job".
   - It costs nothing and works offline.

3. **Let the AI research roles that no list holds.**
   - Yes adds a "Research this role" button that runs one web search.
   - A role counts once two independent websites name it, or one official site.
   - It costs about 1–10p per role, depending on the model and how many pages it reads.

4. **Make options and routes work for any job, marked "not reviewed".**
   - Yes: you can add a course or funding page yourself. Later, the app researches options for
     any job.
   - No: routes stay limited to the 4 reviewed jobs, and the app says so when you pick a target.

## How we work

5. **Use Muse Wonder in your own live sessions.**
   - I've taken "human testing" to mean you using the live app with your own details.
   - Yes: Wonder is tested first, for under 40p. Then your sessions run on it, with a limit of
     about £4 a session.
   - Every call's cost goes into the cost log.
   - Automated tests keep the cheap model.

6. **Shorter review loops.**
   - Yes: small issues wait for the final review.
   - Every sentence on screen says what Careerwright did, never what a list contains.
   - Wording, matching and looks stop after 3 rounds. Then I change the rule or ask you one
     question.
   - You judge suggestion quality on real examples.

7. **Shorter sessions for you.**
   - Yes: the next session is about 30 minutes in the real app, with about 5 questions.
   - The later sessions become one 1-hour session, where you go from the start to a printed
     plan.

8. **Rulebook wording.**
   - Yes approves the four short edits to the agents' rulebook (section 7 of the full report),
     applied with the plan.
