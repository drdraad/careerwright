# Session W1: the product owner's answers

Held 2026-10-30. Written down word for word as given. Anything not listed here is not yet
answered.

## Question 1: which look, or which mix

In the product owner's words: "wow, so this is a tough choice for me. I like all 3, but I feel
like growth and circuit are a bit too chaotic maybe? Maybe the jobs need to be aligned in both?
Horizon is the most logical, but not quite right yet. The animations aren't as good as the
others, and its a bit too moisy with the dashed lines."

Asked to settle it, the product owner chose "Horizon, made livelier": keep Horizon's calm,
lined-up layout, give it animation as alive as Growth and Circuit, and swap the dashed lines
for quieter solid trails. It is built straight into the app and reviewed at session W2.

## Question 4: sound

In the product owner's words: "YEs, all three, but I want them subtle and evocative, not
cheesy!" So all three sounds are built in (the swell when your light comes on, the chime when
a new connection lights up, the tick when you pick a skill), off until the person turns them
on.
