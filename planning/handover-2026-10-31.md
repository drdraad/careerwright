## 7. The loop (what has worked)
1. BASE = `git rev-parse --short HEAD`. Write a dispatch file; dispatch one Opus implementer in
   the background pointing at it. Ledger the dispatch and the agent id. One implementer at a
   time.
2. On report: `review-pkg.sh BASE HEAD`. Large or mixed diffs: two Opus reviewers in parallel,
   split by focus (design and experience; app, truth and tests).
3. Rule on findings, write a fix-round file, dispatch a fresh Opus implementer (originals reach
   500–830k tokens), then a scoped re-review. At round 5, adjudicate and rule; don't loop.
4. Two failed fixes in one direction: change the design, don't patch again. Three rounds in
   W2B each found a new false claim about ESCO; the fix was a rule ("a note says what the
   search did, never what ESCO contains"), not a fourth wording.
5. Ledger every step and ruling as "Ruling: what — why — cost if wrong".
