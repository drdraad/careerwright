The product owner, 2026-11-01: "I don't want to over-rely on existing skills in the process. Many users will be final year school students who have never had a job before."

**Why:** the build so far centres the person's current role (the first question asks for a job title; the slate joins suggestions by the role's ESCO essential skills; the overlooked strength and Skills chapter lean on work experience). All five M0 personas are adults with work history, so tests never exercised a no-job path.

**How to apply:** every chapter must work, and feel like progress, for someone with no job: interests, school subjects, activities, values and constraints carry discovery; a skipped job title is never a dead end or an empty "your skills" failure; job-specific inputs (seniority, industry, CV) stay optional. Check new work and reviews against a school-leaver path, not only a career changer. Related: [[role-space-beyond-standard-lists]], [[nessa-l0-build]].
