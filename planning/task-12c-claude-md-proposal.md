# Task 12c: proposed words for CLAUDE.md (for the product owner's approval)

Nothing in `CLAUDE.md` has changed. If you approve, one sentence in the "Planning (ADR 0013)"
bullet (under "Non-obvious invariants") is replaced.

## Today

> Only an approved award counts unconditionally; a loan is never support.

## Proposed

> Only an approved award counts unconditionally; a loan is never support. One L0 exception
> (spec section 22): a tuition fee loan paid straight to the provider, which the person states,
> is a loan to repay: the fee it pays is in neither their cash, their direct spending nor their
> payment cap, the route lists it as a loan to repay (its repayment unknown, never 0), the
> economic cost still counts the fee once, and one not yet approved makes the route
> conditional beside the self-paid one; every other loan stays information.

## Why

The rule "a loan is never support" still holds, and the loan is still never support. But a
reader who stops at that sentence would count a university fee paid by a tuition fee loan as
the student's own money, which is the rule you changed on 1 November 2026.
